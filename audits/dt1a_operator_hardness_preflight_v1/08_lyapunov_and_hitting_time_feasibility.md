# Lyapunov and hitting-time feasibility

## What DT1-A1 could support

The frozen matched panel can estimate a population-averaged, one-step operator association under C05 parents and the deterministic C05 seed schedule:

`E_sample[h5(X') - h5(X) | operator=o, accepted]`

and scheduled-attempt hard-hit yield for `H5={x:h5(x)>=0.12629708954782198}`. It can also estimate whether hard children survive the frozen Elite-QD plus global-fill selection.

This would be useful evidence for three later candidates:

- local hardness-improvement drift in specified QD4 regions;
- finite-panel hard-region hitting probability;
- empirical selection retention conditional on generation.

## What it cannot establish

- One deterministic attempt per parent/operator does not identify an operator's statewise expectation over mutation randomness.
- A positive panel-average rate is not a uniform lower bound `p_o>0` over all states.
- Conditional-on-valid improvement omits rejection mass; it cannot alone define drift for the actual transition kernel.
- T05 survival mixes HGB ranking, niche occupancy, and global fill; it is not mutation reachability.
- Raw implosion/compression contraction does not imply decreasing or increasing BQ loss after repair.
- Finite one-step evidence supplies neither recurrence, a stopping condition, multi-step dependence, nor a supermartingale argument.

Therefore even a positive DT1-A1 result is only a candidate local bridge/hitting lemma signal. A Lyapunov claim would additionally need a precisely defined state/process, repeated draws or justified kernel assumptions, rejection/self-loop semantics, selection in the transition, and a verified drift or hit bound over its declared domain. If only geometry contracts, BLADE-specific convergence remains unsupported.

## Decision rule after future labels

- If no operator has stable positive scheduled-attempt improvement or hard-hit lift after multiplicity control, stop theory escalation.
- If effects occur only in sparse structural regions, state a region-restricted empirical result and design independent confirmation.
- If effects and retention are stable, design a separate repeated-draw/local-kernel study; do not promote DT1-A1 itself to a theorem.
