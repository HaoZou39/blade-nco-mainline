# Theoretical implications

## A. Local operator-specific improvement

Observed: nine operators had positive pilot mean `delta_h`; implosion and compression had marginal parent-bootstrap intervals above zero. Statistical evidence for differences between operators is not established: the omnibus test and all Holm-adjusted pairwise families were nonsignificant. A defensible future statement is conditional and finite-population: under the frozen C05 parent panel and seed schedule, some operators may have positive average one-step improvement. This is not a statewise drift theorem.

## B. Local hard-region hitting

Observed: 62/330 scheduled attempts hit `h5>=0.12629708954782198`, with operator conditional rates from 0.100 to 0.333. These data support estimating finite-panel hit probabilities in the full 300-parent evaluation. One mutation draw per state/operator cannot establish a uniform positive lower bound over states or randomness.

## C. Selection-conditioned retention

Observed: only 2/62 hard hits and 8/185 improving children entered T05. This motivates a separate selection-retention question and shows that reachability is not retention. It does not identify the counterfactual effect of Elite-QD or global fill.

## D. Structural conditions

Observed: the parent/operator fixed-effect `delta_phi` slope interval crossed zero; QD-region cells were too sparse; one exploratory grid correlation was negative. A full panel may study local structural conditions, but the pilot supplies no stable general rule.

## Evidence ladder

1. Empirical difference: the recorded operator summaries and matched outcomes.
2. Statistical evidence: parent-level intervals, omnibus permutations, and multiplicity-adjusted contrasts.
3. Conditional mathematical statement: possible only over an explicitly bounded C05/QD region and transition convention, after larger-sample confirmation.
4. Proven convergence theorem: not supplied. Recurrence, repeated mutation randomness, rejection/self-loops, multi-step selection, and a uniform drift/hit condition remain unproved.
