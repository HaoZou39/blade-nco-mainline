# Multiobjective Problem Definition

Population-level objectives are higher solver-relative predicted hardness and lower TSPLIB48 structural distance, with frozen QD niches controlling concentration. No weighted hardness–OT fitness, NSGA method, or claimed global Pareto front is used. Arm A exactly replays the original one-elite-plus-global-fill rule. For quota blending only, its realized niche counts define an exact-equivalent `p_base`: selecting the top HGB `q_k` within each niche reproduces A on this fixed pool. This candidate/HGB-dependent representation is explicitly an offline equivalent, not a new claim that the original algorithm sampled from a fixed probability distribution. Arms B/C/D share lambda=.5 quotas; only their within-niche rule changes. Arm E is the preregistered lambda=1 endpoint.
