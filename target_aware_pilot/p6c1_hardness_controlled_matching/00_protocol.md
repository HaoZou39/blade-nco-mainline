# P6-C1 Frozen Protocol

C0 and C1 use the byte-identical unique E0∪E1 candidate union. C0 minimizes frozen P6-A Top5 OT only. C1 minimizes the same objective with exact pooled-E0-boundary, replicate-specific E0 hardness-decile quotas. Hardness is a constraint, never a weighted reward.

- Pre-registered joint feasibility: every replicate must have C1/E1 OT ≤ 1.20, exact 48-reference and ten-decile quotas, integral optimal solution, complete labels, and C0/C1 decile TV ≥ 0.05.
- This operational TV threshold was frozen before solving and means at least 500 of 10,000 memberships must move between hardness bins.
- No training is allowed until all three replicates pass. No tolerance, bin, quota, or objective adjustment is allowed after inspection.
